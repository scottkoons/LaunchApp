## Design Context

### Users

Scott uses Launch throughout his desktop workday to organize tasks, see deadlines, collect reference material, and prepare printed meeting agendas. On the phone, quick notes, dictation through the keyboard, and photo capture come first.

### Brand Personality

Calm, practical, focused. The experience should feel polished and familiar, with clear controls and brief, visible motion.

### Aesthetic Direction

Scott's September 10, 2026 dashboard references now guide all three modes: clean sans-serif headings, rounded outlined controls, thin blue-gray panel borders, and restrained hover/press states. Scott prefers the original Dark palette: charcoal surfaces with warm amber accents and buttons. Keep the newer DM Sans headings and rounded controls. The app must fill the window edge to edge, with no inset frame, light outer border, or colored rim. Space retains navy and warm amber; Light uses cool pale surfaces and ink-blue controls. Use the existing locally served DM Sans across the interface to match the references. Preserve the wide, two-column task creation modal and compact task rows. Use the approved Orbit rocket with Launch and Task Organizer. Avoid clip art, excessive empty space, and decorative dashboard elements.

### iPhone Liquid Display

Scott's September 24, 2026 request adds Liquid Display as the iPhone default. Use native Apple system typography, translucent navigation surfaces, a floating bottom tab bar, spacious touch controls, and solid readable content. Follow the device's light/dark appearance. Keep Space, Dark, Light, and every existing task view available. Remember subsequent iPhone appearance choices separately from desktop preferences. Existing appearances retain their established design.

### Design Principles

- Keep frequent actions immediate and in predictable positions.
- Group related controls and maintain clear alignment.
- Make task and reference content easy to scan without unnecessary containers.
- Preserve phone capture, keyboard access, reduced motion, and accessible labels.
- Refine existing workflows without adding complexity.
